## What Was Created

A Kotlin-based cognitive substrate architecture centered on protected semantic
lanes, contradiction resolution, deliberative consensus, prompt-stream immune
defense, and auditable execution receipts.

- Judge / Benchmark Harnesses

## Why It Was Created

To address recurring failures in language-model workflows: hallucination,
semantic drift, contradiction persistence, policy degradation, and
prompt-injection susceptibility. Goal was cognitive infrastructure and
governance, not just another inference wrapper.

## Design Rationale

Protected semantic lanes preserve hierarchy between axioms, enterprise policy,
factual knowledge, and transient memory. Contradiction handling isolated as an
explicit auditable subsystem. Immune layer detects prompt injection before state
contamination propagates.

## Alternatives Rejected

- Single flat memory model: blurs protected and transient states
- Purely statistical contradiction handling: no auditable resolution trail
