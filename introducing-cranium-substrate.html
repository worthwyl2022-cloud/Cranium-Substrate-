WorthWyl Media · Las Vegas, NV First publication: 2026-08-29 The Problem Why cognitive infrastructure matters Standard language-model workflows bury contradiction, hallucination, and policy drift inside opaque generation processes. Cranium Substrate separates protected axioms, enterprise policy, factual material, working memory, and hypothetical branches into bounded lanes so conflict can be detected, audited, and resolved explicitly — not hoped away. Architecture Core modules 01 Canon Lanes Priority-bound semantic channels separating axiomatic, policy, factual, preference, working, and hypothetical knowledge domains. 02 Cognitive Atoms Immutable epistemic units carrying proposition, lane, provenance, confidence score, timestamp, and optional embedding. 03 Contradiction Engine Pairwise semantic conflict detection with configurable resolution strategies — lock, supersede, or escalate. 04 Deliberation Engine Iterative consensus loop that resolves conflict and computes convergence stability across the working atom pool. 05 Epistemic Immune Layer Prompt-stream defense scanning for jailbreak patterns, override attempts, and direct axiom-negation vectors. 06 Audit & Receipts Reproducible benchmark harnesses, JSON receipt generation, and audit-report pipeline for transparent evaluation.
